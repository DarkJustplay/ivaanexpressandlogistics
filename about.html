Our Mission

Built to Move India Forward

Ivaan Express was founded in 2022 with a singular promise — get shipments where they need to be, on time, every time. What started as three trucks and two routes out of Delhi NCR has become one of India's most trusted freight and express logistics networks.

We believe in infrastructure that works silently and reliably — not just in metros, but in the districts, tehsils, and towns that form the real spine of Indian commerce. Our live tracking portal, launched in 2023, was the first of its kind in our segment — giving every customer full shipment visibility from booking to doorstep delivery.

Today, we operate across air cargo, surface freight, and last-mile delivery — with a single integrated platform tying it all together. Because logistics shouldn't be complicated. It should just work.
